<p>LyricPost turns your favourite lines from a song into a stylish lyrics card you can save or share in seconds.</p>
<ul>
<li>Search any song and pick it from the results with its album cover</li>
<li>Lyrics are loaded automatically from LRCLIB, the free and open lyrics database, or type your own</li>
<li>Select the lines you want and style the card: colours, blur, fonts, spacing, corner radius and layout presets</li>
<li>Save the image to your Gallery in high resolution</li>
<li>Share it straight to Instagram (Feed, Stories, Chats), WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Snapchat and any other installed app</li>
<li>Light and dark theme</li>
</ul>
<p>No account, no ads, no tracking.</p>
<p>Song search uses the Last.fm API, lyrics come from LRCLIB and album covers from the Cover Art Archive, so an internet connection is needed to find songs.</p>
<p>Based on the LyricPost web app by Adam Palinkiewicz (MIT licence).</p>
